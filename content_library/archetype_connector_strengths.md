Your gift is creating value for others without keeping score. People trust the introductions you make because they're genuine — you connect people who will truly benefit from knowing each other, not interchangeable names. That same instinct makes you good at fostering real relationships: once you connect two people, you stay invested in how that connection grows. Those who are more reserved especially benefit from knowing you, since you do the connecting work they might hesitate to do themselves.

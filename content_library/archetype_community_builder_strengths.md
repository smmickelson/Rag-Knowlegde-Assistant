Your strength is creating belonging — people feel included and seen in groups you're part of. You tend to be well-connected within your communities, the kind of person who's "in the know" and likely to know who's who. That engagement compounds: showing up consistently in the same spaces builds you a reputation that makes new opportunities and connections come to you, rather than the other way around.

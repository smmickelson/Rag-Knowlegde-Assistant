A clear, confident introduction is a skill, not a personality trait — it gets easier with repetition. Write one or two sentences that cover who you are and what you're focused on right now, then say them out loud until they feel natural rather than rehearsed. Use the same version a few times in a row before changing it; consistency is what makes it feel confident, not cleverness.

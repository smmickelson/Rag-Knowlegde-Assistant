Your strength is in the tiny details most people forget — a name, a detail about someone's family, the thing they mentioned once in passing. You remember it, and you bring it up again later, which is why people feel genuinely seen around you. That consistency is rare: the people in your life know you'll follow up, check in, and remember, even years later. It's a kind of loyalty that's hard to fake and even harder to replace.

You see relationships as a web, not a ladder. When something in a conversation signals that two people should know each other, you notice it — and you're genuinely excited to make the introduction, not because it benefits you, but because the connection itself feels worth creating. You may lean more extroverted, drawing energy from people, though that's a tendency, not a requirement. What drives you is curiosity about who else someone should meet.

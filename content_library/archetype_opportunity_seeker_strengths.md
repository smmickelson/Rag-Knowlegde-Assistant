What sets you apart is initiative — you put yourself in front of opportunity instead of waiting for it to find you. That instinct toward visibility means you tend to be early to notice what's happening and quick to show up for it, whether that's an event, a project, or a conversation. Real engagement and simple participation are different things, and your strength is leaning toward the former: being present with intention, not attendance for its own sake.

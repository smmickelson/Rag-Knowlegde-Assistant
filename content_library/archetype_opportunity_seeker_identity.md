Your instinct is to be where things are happening rather than wait to be invited. Sitting on the sidelines doesn't sit right with you — you'd rather be in the room, even if "in the room" looks different depending on the day: sometimes that's working the crowd, sometimes it's standing quietly nearby and noticing what's going on. You may be more extroverted or more reserved — either way, the pull toward visibility and participation is the same.

You're drawn to bringing people together around something bigger than any one relationship — a team, an organization, a cause. Showing up is about more than being seen; it's about helping a group feel stronger because you're in it. You tend to be thoughtful about where you invest your time and energy, choosing organizations and events with some strategy rather than attending everything. You may lean more extroverted, energized by active participation in a group.

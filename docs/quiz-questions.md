# Quiz questions

Source: the existing quiz at tools.werkitgirls.com, copied word for word. Do not reorder the questions: the scorer maps each question to styles by its number (see src/scoring.py).

Answer choices, the same for every question, and the value each one sends to the scorer:

| Choice | Value |
|---|---|
| Never | 1 |
| Occasionally | 2 |
| Frequently | 3 |
| Always | 4 |

Questions:

1. I clearly and confidently introduce myself in professional settings.
2. I regularly reach out to my network to share updates or useful information.
3. I actively participate in professional or community organizations.
4. I proactively connect people who could benefit from knowing each other.
5. I make an effort to remember key details about people.
6. I attend events to build connections.
7. I follow up with gratitude when someone shares time, information, or resources.
8. I actively seek to expand my network across diverse perspectives and backgrounds.
9. I respond to messages within 24–48 hours.
10. I support others in building their networks.
11. I engage in company or organizational social events.
12. I proactively reintroduce myself rather than assuming others remember me.
13. I volunteer for cross-functional initiatives to build new relationships.
14. I ask my network for advice, insights, or support when needed.
15. I intentionally think about how to grow and strengthen my network over time.
